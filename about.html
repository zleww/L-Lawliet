About L-Lawliet.

A student project that explains Philippine Republic Acts in plain English.

What it is

Most people only hear about a law when it is on the news. L-Lawliet takes the Republic Acts that come up most often in daily life and rewrites each one as a short summary: what it requires, who it covers, an example and the penalties.

Anyone can read the first 10 laws. With a free account you can read all of them and leave notes for other readers. The same database also runs JurisCards, a flashcard site for studying these laws.

Where the text comes from

Each summary is written from the law as published in the Official Gazette of the Philippines.

The summaries are simplified, so they are a starting point and not legal advice. Each one links to the full text in the Official Gazette, which is the version that counts.
