RA 10175: Online Safety & Anti-Cybercrime Law

Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012

Summary

Protects people from online hacking, identity theft, cyber-bullying, and online libel.

Who it covers

All internet and social media users, businesses, and website owners.

Example

If someone hacks your Facebook account or posts false malicious rumors about you online, they can be prosecuted under this law.

Penalties

Fines from PHP 200,000 and 6 to 12+ years in prison, depending on severity.
