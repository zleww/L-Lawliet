Philippine Legal System

Understanding the foundations of Philippine law.

The Philippine legal system is shaped by the Constitution, statutes enacted by Congress, administrative regulations, judicial decisions, and established legal principles. Understanding how these sources interact is fundamental to the study and practice of law.

01 / CONSTITUTION

1987 Constitution

The fundamental and supreme law of the Philippines. It establishes the structure of government and protects the fundamental rights of the people.
